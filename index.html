A scientist.
An educator.

I am a Maître de conférences at Université Paris Cité, Faculty of Pharmacy. My research connects cellular and molecular neuroscience with systems neuroscience and quantitative approaches to behavior.

I teach cell biology and enjoy helping students develop the critical thinking, experimental reasoning and communication skills that make science possible.
